What is English Immersion?

A playful, welcoming way to experience English outside the classroom. Through books, stories and creative activities, participants discover the language naturally, build confidence and develop a love of English.

A Welcoming, Community-Led Program

A love of language, books and connection.

Led by a Native English-Speaking Facilitator

A warm, engaging and supportive learning environment.

Located in Albi

At Uma’s Books and Adventures, a unique space where books, community and learning meet.
