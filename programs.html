English Immersion Program

A joyful start for curious minds.

One hour a week in a fun, friendly and supportive environment. Our English immersion program is a gentle, progressive way for children to learn English — a great complement to English courses or a wonderful way to start.

Through stories, games, activities and creative play, children discover the language naturally while building confidence.
