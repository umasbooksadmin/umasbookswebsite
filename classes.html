Éveil English Immersion Program

Little Readers. A Bigger World.

A joyful, play-based program for children to discover books, language and the world through stories, songs and creative activities — all in English.

Program Details

Who?
Children 18 months – 5 years
When?
Wednesdays at 11:00
Saturdays at 14:00
Choose one date and time for the year, when pre-registering. Your child will attend this session each week.
Where?
Uma's Books
Rue Peyrolière, 81000 Albi
Price
€20 per session
Paid monthly. One session per week (your child's reserved spot).
Format
Drop-off program — no adults accompany children. Our experienced facilitators provide a warm, safe and nurturing environment.
Start Date
Program begins in November and runs all year, including during school holidays.
Good to know…
  • The first session is free!
  • Programs are €20 per session (paid monthly).
  • One session per week — you must choose the date and time when pre-registering.
  • Cancellations must be made at least 48 hours in advance.
  • Programs run during school holidays.
  • No adults accompany children during the program.
