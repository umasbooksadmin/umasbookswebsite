Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026.

Uma’s Books & Adventures ("we", "us", "our") is a non-profit association based in Albi, France. This page explains what personal information we collect through this website, why, and what rights you have over it. If anything here is unclear, email us at admin@umasbooks.org.

Who we are

Uma’s Books & Adventures is the data controller for the information described below. We’re a volunteer-run, non-profit secondhand English-language bookshop and community space in Albi. Postal address: Uma’s Books & Adventures, Rue Peyrolière, 81000 Albi, France.

What we collect, and why

We only collect what we need to run the shop, our programs and our community, and only from what you give us directly:

  • Account details (email, name, password) — if you create a member account, to let you log in, keep favorites, and access member features.
  • Orders and reservations (name, email, phone, what you bought or reserved) — to fulfil a book order or hold a reservation for pickup.
  • Payment information — online payments (memberships, drop-in sessions, book orders) are handled entirely by our payment processor, HelloAsso; we never see or store your card details ourselves.
  • Event and session sign-ups (name, email) — to manage attendance and capacity.
  • Membership, volunteer, wishlist, pre-registration and contact-form messages (name, email, phone, and whatever you write) — several of these forms open your own email application and are sent by you, directly to us; we only store what actually reaches our inbox or database, not what you merely typed and didn't send.
  • Community content (forum posts, charity votes) — visible to other members if you choose to post; tied to your account.

Cookies and local storage

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in and remember your cart (strictly necessary — the site can't function without it), and your browser's local storage to remember your language choice (EN/FR) and, if you're not logged in, which books you've favorited on this device. None of this is used for advertising or tracking, and nothing is shared with ad networks.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information to anyone. We share it only with the services that help us run the site: HelloAsso (payment processing), our email provider (to send confirmations and replies), and Render (our hosting provider, which stores our database). Each only receives what it needs to do its job.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account or an active relationship with us (e.g. recent orders or reservations), or as long as French law requires for accounting records, and delete or anonymize it afterwards. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time — see "Your rights" below.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and French data protection law, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict how we use it. To exercise any of these rights, email admin@umasbooks.org — we'll respond within a reasonable time.

If you believe we haven't handled your information properly, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with France's data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Children

Our English Immersion program is for children as well as adults. When a parent or guardian registers a child, they're providing that information with parental consent and responsibility for its accuracy.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information — passwords are stored encrypted, never in plain text, and connections to this site are encrypted (HTTPS). No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we don't collect more than we need, precisely to limit what would ever be at risk.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: admin@umasbooks.org.
