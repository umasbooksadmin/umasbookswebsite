Volunteer With Us

Uma's is entirely volunteer-run — every hour on the shop floor, every sorted donation, every event, is someone giving their time. New volunteers, at any language level, are always welcome.

Shop Floor Welcome visitors and mind the till during opening hours.
Sorting & Shelving Sort donations and keep the collection organised.
Events & Language Exchange Help run book club, language exchange, or story swaps.
Bilingual Support Help visitors and paperwork move smoothly between English and French.
