Membership Terms

Last updated: September 2026.

These are the terms of membership at Uma’s Books & Adventures, a non-profit association in Albi, France. By registering for membership, you agree to them.

What membership is

Membership is a one-time 20€ contribution that supports our non-profit — the shop, our English programs, and community events — and gives you a say in how part of what we raise is used. It is not a purchase of goods or a subscription service.

Benefits

Members can vote on the charity our association supports each quarter, join the members' discussion forum, and get access to special events, engagements, and a member tote bag. We may add, change, or retire specific benefits from time to time as the association's activities evolve — see the Membership page for the current list.

Joining and payment

Registering sends us your details; we review each request before confirming membership and reaching out with payment instructions. Membership only takes effect once the 20€ contribution has been received — registering alone does not make you a member.

Community conduct

The discussion forum and community spaces are here to connect people over books and shared interests. We ask that members treat each other with respect — no harassment, hate speech, spam, or illegal content. We may remove content or suspend a member's access for violating this, particularly where it affects other members' or volunteers' safety or wellbeing.

Ending your membership

You can ask us to close your membership and account at any time by emailing admin@umasbooks.org. The one-time contribution already made isn't refundable, since it directly supports activities already under way, except where required by law.

Your data

Membership registration collects your name, email, phone number and any notes you add. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle this information.

Changes to these terms

If we make significant changes to these terms, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where practical, let current members know.

Contact

Questions about membership: admin@umasbooks.org.
